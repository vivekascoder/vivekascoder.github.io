- I try to lift 5-6 times a day.
- Actively track my lifts every day using [TrackMeDaddy](https://trackmedaddy.vivek.ink/). Reason being once you become an intermediate lifter (training actively for more than 3 years). It's not as easy as used to be to make progress. And especially if you get other important things in life, lifting becomes secondary and if you don't track you won't be able to quantify your **intensity** of sets. Getting insights on your intensity, Volume each day helps you be more consistent with making progress.
- **Small Injury**: I've been going thru triceps tendonitis for 2-3 months not and my progress of pushing movements has gone down a lot. But this also helping me to focus on weaker areaf (rear dealt, lower lats, etc for me).
- I've followed a few splits over the cource of years, RN I'm sticking to a 5-6 days (either push-pull-leg or push pull leg, rest, upper lower type split).
