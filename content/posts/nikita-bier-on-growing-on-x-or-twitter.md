## Short Summary

Nikita's advice is pretty consistent: X rewards original, native, high-signal content that gives value inside the timeline itself. He is especially bullish on domain expertise, thoughtful posting formats, native video with captions, and using X as a reputation engine that opens real-world opportunities rather than just chasing vanity metrics.

## Core Tips

## 1. Build for reputation, not just impressions

- He repeatedly frames an X account as valuable for the doors it opens offline: jobs, consulting, status, and relationships.
- High-quality followers and credibility in the right niche matter more than raw follower count.

## 2. Become known for one domain

- His clearest growth advice is to become a thought leader in a specific domain instead of building a generic mass-market meme/news account.
- He explicitly says niche authority has "unlimited upside" while broad mass-market posting turns you into a cheap media property.
- He also says quality of audience matters more than topline likes.

## 9. Post natively to X first when possible

- In replies about creator incentives, he says posting on X first matters because they can match the original and still pay the creator even if discovery happens elsewhere.
- Practical tactic: if you cross-post, publish to X first or at least simultaneously.

## 10. Protect early replies from spam

- He mentioned that he sets replies to in-network only for the first 20 minutes to block bots.
- That's a tactical posting habit, not a platform-wide rule, but it is a useful operational tip for bigger accounts or controversial posts.

## 11. Do not optimize for vanity reach if the audience quality is wrong

- This is the same theme as niche authority, but worth separating: he cares about who is seeing and liking the content, not just how many.
- That's especially relevant if you're using X to get jobs, customers, investors, or consulting work.
